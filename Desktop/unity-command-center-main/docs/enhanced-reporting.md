# Enhanced Reporting System - Comprehensive Field Coverage

## 🎯 **Overview**
A state-of-the-art reporting system that captures comprehensive information while maintaining user privacy and safety. This enhanced form covers all aspects of GBV reporting with intelligent field validation and progressive disclosure.

## 📋 **Reporting Fields & Categories**

### **1. Basic Incident Information**
#### **Incident Type Classification**
- **Physical Violence**: Domestic Violence, Assault, Child Abuse, Elder Abuse
- **Emotional Abuse**: Verbal Abuse, Psychological Abuse, Coercive Control, Harassment
- **Sexual Violence**: Rape, Sexual Assault, Sexual Harassment, Child Sexual Abuse
- **Economic Violence**: Financial Abuse, Property Damage, Employment Discrimination, Inheritance Dispute
- **Online/Cyber**: Cyberstalking, Online Harassment, Revenge Porn, Online Threats
- **Other**: Human Trafficking, Forced Marriage, Honor-Based Violence

#### **Urgency Level Assessment**
- **Immediate Danger**: Life-threatening situations requiring emergency response
- **Urgent**: Need help within hours
- **High Priority**: Need help within 24 hours
- **Medium Priority**: Need help within 3 days
- **Low Priority**: Need help within a week

### **2. Location Information**
#### **Geographic Details**
- **Province Selection**: All 10 Zambian provinces
- **District Selection**: Dynamic districts based on selected province
- **Specific Location**: Street address, house number, building name
- **Nearby Landmark**: Schools, markets, churches, hospitals for easier identification

#### **Location Intelligence**
- GPS coordinates (automatically captured)
- Location accuracy verification
- Safe location identification
- Emergency proximity assessment

### **3. Temporal Information**
#### **Time-Based Details**
- **Incident Date**: Calendar selection with validation
- **Incident Time**: Time picker for precise timing
- **Ongoing Status**: Checkbox for currently occurring incidents
- **Duration Estimation**: For ongoing or repeated incidents

#### **Temporal Analysis**
- Pattern detection for repeated incidents
- Time-based response prioritization
- Statute of limitations considerations
- Evidence preservation timing

### **4. Detailed Description**
#### **Narrative Field**
- **Rich Text Description**: Up to 500 characters with character counter
- **Structured Prompts**: Guided questions for comprehensive reporting
- **Evidence References**: Ability to reference specific evidence
- **Impact Assessment**: How the incident affected the victim

#### **Description Analysis**
- AI-powered sentiment analysis
- Keyword extraction for categorization
- Severity assessment algorithms
- Response team routing logic

### **5. Evidence Management**
#### **Evidence Types**
- **Photos/Videos**: Visual documentation
- **Messages/Emails**: Digital communication evidence
- **Documents**: Official papers, medical records
- **Audio Recordings**: Voice recordings, phone calls
- **Witnesses**: People who saw or heard something
- **Other Evidence**: Miscellaneous supporting materials

#### **Evidence Handling**
- Secure upload mechanisms
- Evidence preservation protocols
- Chain of custody maintenance
- Privacy protection for sensitive content

### **6. Witness Information**
#### **Witness Assessment**
- **No Witnesses**: Incident occurred in private
- **1 Witness**: Single observer
- **2-3 Witnesses**: Small group observation
- **4+ Witnesses**: Public incident
- **Not Sure**: Uncertain about witness presence

#### **Witness Protection**
- Anonymous reporting options
- Witness safety assessment
- Protected testimony procedures
- Witness support services

### **7. Safety Assessment**
#### **Current Safety Level**
- **Safe**: Currently in a secure location
- **Unsafe**: In immediate danger
- **Uncertain**: Unsure about safety status
- **Monitored**: Being watched or controlled

#### **Safety Planning**
- **Immediate Help Request**: Emergency services activation
- **Safe Location**: Identification of secure places
- **Safety Planning**: Step-by-step safety strategies
- **Risk Assessment**: Danger level evaluation

### **8. Support Services**
#### **Service Requirements**
- **Counseling**: Mental health and emotional support
- **Medical Care**: Physical health treatment
- **Legal Assistance**: Legal advice and representation
- **Shelter**: Safe housing options

#### **Support Coordination**
- Service provider matching
- Appointment scheduling
- Transportation arrangements
- Follow-up care coordination

### **9. Contact Preferences**
#### **Communication Methods**
- **Phone Call**: Direct voice communication
- **SMS/Text**: Text message updates
- **Email**: Written communication
- **WhatsApp**: Encrypted messaging
- **No Contact**: Anonymous reporting

#### **Contact Timing**
- **Any Time**: 24/7 availability
- **Morning**: 8AM - 12PM
- **Afternoon**: 12PM - 5PM
- **Evening**: 5PM - 8PM
- **Night**: 8PM - 10PM

### **10. Victim Information (Optional)**
#### **Demographics**
- **Age Range**: For appropriate service matching
- **Gender**: For culturally appropriate response
- **Relationship**: Connection to perpetrator (if known)

#### **Privacy Protection**
- All victim information is optional
- Anonymous reporting available
- Data encryption and protection
- Limited access protocols

### **11. Perpetrator Information (Optional)**
#### **Identification**
- **Known/Unknown**: Whether perpetrator is identifiable
- **Relationship**: Connection to victim
- **Description**: Physical or identifying details
- **Pattern**: History of abusive behavior

#### **Safety Considerations**
- Risk assessment based on perpetrator profile
- Safety planning for perpetrator encounters
- Legal action coordination
- Protection order assistance
